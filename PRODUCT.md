# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers who want to improve their resumes and practice interviews before applying. This includes fresh graduates, career switchers, internship seekers, and experienced candidates preparing for a new role.

## Product Purpose

The platform helps users turn raw resume information into a stronger job application, then rehearse role-specific interviews. Success means a user can create a resume, choose a target role, receive AI diagnosis and rewrite suggestions, practice interview questions, and review feedback in one coherent workflow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, professional, encouraging.

The product should feel like a polished AI career coach: focused enough for serious job preparation, friendly enough for repeated practice, and refined enough to feel trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Do not make it look like a generic school management system, a noisy admin dashboard, or a purple-gradient AI landing page. Avoid template-like SaaS cards, oversized marketing hero sections, decorative glow effects, and dense table-first layouts.

Magic Resume is the visual reference for the resume editor surface: clean three-column workspace, thin borders, white tool panels, compact icon actions, and a realistic resume preview.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the resume visible while the user edits or improves it.
2. Make AI feedback specific, structured, and directly actionable.
3. Treat interview practice as a guided workflow, not a chat gimmick.
4. Use calm hierarchy and precise spacing so the product feels professional.
5. Preserve course-design clarity: every major AI result should have a record that can be stored in MySQL.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-friendly controls. Keep motion subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone for scores, warnings, or status labels.
